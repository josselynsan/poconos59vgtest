Things to Do

Pocono Mountains provide a picturesque backdrop where you can spend time in the region’s historic rural towns while also exploring nature. Experience 365 days of seasonal activities, adventurous activities such as water parks, train tours, zip lines, hiking trails, and horseback riding stables.

Nature Exploration

Delaware National Gap Reserve, Promised Land State Park, and Bushkill Falls, a series of eight cascading waterfalls known as the “Niagara of Pennsylvania,” which are popular outdoor destinations in the Pocono Mountains. These sites are perfect for those that love hiking, fishing, birdwatching, and nature enthusiasts.

Zip and Slide

Pocono Tree Ventures features an aerial ropes course with over 90 elements up to 40 ft. high. Blue/White Lightning Tubing at Pocono Mountain Villas offers year-round snow tubing with a Magic Carpet lift and two 400-foot lanes. Pocono Hills Golf Course, designed by Nicolas Psiahas, is an 18-hole, par-71 course known for its challenging greens. Bushkill Riding Stables provides easy horseback rides for beginners with professional guides.

Family Fun

Explore Frontier Mini Golf and visit the Monroe Farmers Market for local produce. For family fun, Pocono Go-Karts and Play Park offers a 1,400-foot-long adult track, kiddie karts, and miniature golf. Try horseback riding at the Bushkill Riding Stables.

Not So Hidden Gems

Visit Pocono Candles, Destination Earth Paintball and take a ride on the Lehigh Gorge Scenic Railway from Jim Thorpe into Lehigh Gorge State Park. Enjoy a narrated, 16-mile round trip in vintage passenger coaches from as early as 1917, showcasing the Poconos’ natural beauty.
